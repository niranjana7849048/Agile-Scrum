## Impediment Removal Process

1. The developer reports the impediment during the Daily Scrum.
2. The Scrum Master records the impediment in the log.
3. The Scrum Master identifies the responsible person or external team.
4. The Scrum Master follows up until the blocker is resolved.
5. The developer resumes the blocked work after resolution.
6. The impediment status is updated.

## Review

The Scrum Master monitors both impediments and ensures that they do not unnecessarily delay the sprint goal.
