# Backlog Reprioritisation – Digital Wallet Application

## 1. Change Request

On Day 4 of the 10-day sprint, the business requests a new feature:

**Refund-to-Wallet:** Users should receive eligible refunds directly into their digital wallet.

## 2. Impact Analysis

The new requirement was not part of the original sprint backlog.

Adding it directly to the sprint may affect:
- Existing sprint commitments.
- Developer workload.
- Testing activities.
- The team's ability to achieve the Sprint Goal.

## 3. Reprioritised Backlog

| Story ID | User Story | Priority | Story Points | Decision |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| US01 | Secure user login | High | 3 | Retain |
| US02 | View wallet balance | High | 3 | Retain |
| US03 | Add money to wallet | High | 5 | Retain |
| US04 | Transfer money to another wallet | High | 8 | Retain |
| US05 | View transaction history | Medium | 4 | Retain |
| US06 | Download monthly statements | Low | 5 | Keep in Product Backlog |
| US07 | Receive refunds directly into wallet | High | To be estimated | Add to Product Backlog for refinement |

## 4. Decision on the Change Request

The refund-to-wallet feature should first be discussed with the Product Owner and Developers.

If the feature can be added without endangering the Sprint Goal, the Product Owner and Developers can negotiate a scope adjustment.

If it cannot be completed safely within the sprint:
- Add the feature to the Product Backlog.
- Refine its requirements and acceptance criteria.
- Estimate its story points.
- Prioritise it for a future sprint.

## 5. Who Decides?

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Product Owner | Owns Product Backlog ordering and clarifies business priority. |
| Developers | Assess technical effort and decide how much work can be completed. |
| Scrum Master | Facilitates discussion and helps the team follow Scrum practices. |

The Product Owner does not unilaterally force additional work into the Sprint Backlog. The scope is negotiated with the Developers.

## 6. Final Decision

The team retains the original sprint commitment of 23 story points unless the Product Owner and Developers agree on a safe scope adjustment.

The refund-to-wallet feature is added to the Product Backlog for refinement and prioritisation.
