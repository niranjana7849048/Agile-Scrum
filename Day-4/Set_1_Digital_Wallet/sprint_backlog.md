## 5. Sprint Goal

Enable registered users to securely access their digital wallets, view balances, add money, transfer funds to other users, and review transaction history within the 10-day sprint.

## 6. Measurable Success Criteria

1. Users can log in using valid credentials.
2. Users can view their current wallet balance.
3. Successful deposits update the wallet balance correctly.
4. Successful transfers update the sender's and receiver's balances.
5. Completed transactions appear in transaction history.
6. All selected stories satisfy their acceptance criteria and Definition of Done.

## 7. Sprint Commitment

The team commits to completing 5 user stories worth 23 story points, subject to the sprint's capacity and any agreed scope changes.
