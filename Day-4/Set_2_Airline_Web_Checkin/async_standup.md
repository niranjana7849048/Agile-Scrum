# Async Daily Stand-up – Airline Web Check-in

## 1. Overview

The Airline Web Check-in team works across two different time zones. A live Daily Scrum meeting may be difficult for all members to attend.

An asynchronous stand-up allows team members to share their updates through a common communication platform.

## 5. Asynchronous vs Live Stand-up

| Aspect | Live Stand-up | Asynchronous Stand-up |
|---|---|---|
| Communication | Real-time discussion | Written updates |
| Scheduling | Requires a common meeting time | Members post updates at agreed times |
| Time Zones | Difficult for distributed teams | More flexible |
| Clarification | Immediate discussion | Follow-up messages may be needed |
| Documentation | Notes may need to be recorded separately | Updates are automatically documented |
| Interaction | Direct interaction | Mostly text-based |
| Best Use | Co-located teams | Distributed teams |

## 6. Benefits of Async Stand-up

- Supports team members working in different time zones.
- Reduces scheduling difficulties.
- Maintains visibility of sprint progress.
- Creates a written record of updates.
- Helps identify impediments.
- Allows team members to respond during their working hours.

## 7. Conclusion

Asynchronous stand-ups are useful for distributed Scrum teams because they provide flexibility while maintaining transparency.

However, complex blockers and discussions may still require direct conversations or short collaborative meetings.
