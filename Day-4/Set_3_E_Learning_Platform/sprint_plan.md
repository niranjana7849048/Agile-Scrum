## 3. Sprint Capacity and Story Selection

The team selects 24 story points, leaving 6 points of capacity for uncertainty and unexpected work.

## 4. Sprint Goal

Enable learners to register, securely log in, browse available courses, enroll in courses, track their learning progress, and receive course notifications.

## 5. Sprint Success Criteria

1. Learners can successfully register and log in.
2. Available courses are displayed correctly.
3. Learners can enroll in selected courses.
4. Course progress is recorded and displayed.
5. Course notifications are generated correctly.
6. Selected stories satisfy their acceptance criteria and Definition of Done.

## 6. Sprint Commitment

The team plans 24 story points against a historical velocity of 30 points.

The remaining capacity provides flexibility while maintaining focus on the Sprint Goal.

## 7. Conclusion

The sprint focuses on the core learner experience, from registration to course enrollment and progress tracking.
