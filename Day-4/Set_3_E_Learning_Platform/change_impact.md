# Change Impact Analysis – E-Learning Platform

## 1. Change Request

On Day 6 of the 10-day sprint, the Product Owner requests a new feature:

**Certificate Download:** Learners should be able to download certificates after successfully completing a course.

The feature was not included in the original Sprint Backlog.

## 2. Impact Analysis

| Area | Impact |
|---|---|
| Development | Requires certificate generation and download functionality. |
| Testing | Requires testing certificate eligibility and file downloads. |
| Time | Only four working days remain in the sprint. |
| Resources | Developers may need to shift focus from existing stories. |
| Sprint Goal | Uncontrolled addition of work may affect the original Sprint Goal. |

## 3. Scope Change Options

### Option 1 – Swap an Existing Story

- Discuss the new request with the Developers.
- Identify a lower-priority or unfinished story that can be removed.
- Estimate the certificate feature.
- Negotiate the scope adjustment with the Product Owner.

### Option 2 – Defer the Feature

- Add the certificate download feature to the Product Backlog.
- Define acceptance criteria.
- Estimate the effort.
- Prioritise it for a future sprint.

### Option 3 – Protect the Sprint Goal

- Continue working on the existing Sprint Goal.
- Avoid adding work that could put the goal at risk.
- Keep the certificate feature in the Product Backlog for future planning.

## 4. Recommended Decision

The recommended approach is to add the certificate download feature to the Product Backlog and refine it for the next sprint.

Since the request arrives on Day 6, only four working days remain. The team should not accept additional work without assessing its impact.

If the Product Owner and Developers agree that a scope adjustment is feasible without endangering the Sprint Goal, they can negotiate a change to the Sprint Backlog.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Product Owner | Clarifies the business need and orders the Product Backlog. |
| Developers | Assess technical effort and decide how the work can be completed. |
| Scrum Master | Facilitates discussion and helps the team manage the change. |

## 6. Conclusion

The certificate download request should be evaluated based on its business priority, technical effort, and remaining sprint capacity.

The Sprint Goal should remain protected while the team and Product Owner negotiate any changes to scope.
