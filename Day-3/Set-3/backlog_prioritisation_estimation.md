## 3. Planning Poker Discussion

Planning Poker is a collaborative estimation technique in which team members independently select story point values and discuss differences before agreeing on a final estimate.

**Example outlier discussion:**

Story: Book an Appointment

- Initial estimates: 3, 5 and 8 points.
- Discussion: Team members considered slot availability checks, booking confirmation and the possibility of another patient selecting the same slot.
- Final estimate: 5 story points.

The team agreed on 5 points after discussing the complexity and scope of the story.

## 4. Story Points vs Hours

| Story Points | Hours |
|---|---|
| Measure relative effort and complexity. | Measure actual time spent. |
| Consider complexity, uncertainty and effort. | Represent estimated or actual duration. |
| Do not represent a fixed number of hours. | Expressed in hours or minutes. |
| Used for comparing and estimating user stories. | Used for time planning and tracking. |

## 5. Conclusion

The backlog contains 12 user stories with a total estimated size of **38 story points**. Prioritisation based on business value and effort helps the team focus on important features, while Planning Poker supports collaborative estimation.
