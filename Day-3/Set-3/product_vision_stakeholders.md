
# Hospital Appointment Booking Application

## 1. Product Vision

To develop a user-friendly mobile application that allows patients to book, reschedule and cancel doctor appointments conveniently. The application will also provide appointment reminders and access to doctor availability, reducing waiting time and improving the hospital appointment experience.

## 2. Stakeholder Mapping

| Stakeholder | Scrum Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Hospital Management | Product Owner | Defines business goals and prioritises product requirements. |
| Scrum Team Facilitator | Scrum Master | Facilitates Scrum events and removes obstacles. |
| Developers | Developer | Design, develop and test the mobile application. |
| Patients | Customer | Use the application to book, reschedule and cancel appointments. |
| Doctors | Customer | Provide availability and manage appointment schedules. |
| Receptionists | Customer | Assist with appointment coordination and schedule updates. |

## Conclusion

The stakeholder mapping helps identify responsibilities and ensures that the development team considers the needs of patients, doctors and hospital management.
