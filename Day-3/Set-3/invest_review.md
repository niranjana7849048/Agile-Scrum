
# INVEST Review – Hospital Appointment Booking Application

## 1. INVEST Checklist

INVEST stands for:

- **I – Independent:** The story can be developed independently.
- **N – Negotiable:** Details can be discussed and refined.
- **V – Valuable:** The story provides value to the user.
- **E – Estimable:** The effort required can be estimated.
- **S – Small:** The story is small enough to complete within a Sprint.
- **T – Testable:** The story has clear conditions for verification.

## 3. Stories Requiring Improvement

### Story 8 – Receive Appointment Reminder

**Issue:** The reminder timing and delivery condition are not specified, making the story difficult to test.

**Rewritten Story:**

As a patient, I want to receive a reminder notification 24 hours before my confirmed appointment so that I can prepare for my consultation.

### Story 11 – Update Doctor Availability

**Issue:** The term "update availability" is broad and does not clearly define the action.

**Rewritten Story:**

As a doctor, I want to mark specific appointment time slots as available so that patients can book those slots.

## 4. Conclusion

The INVEST checklist helps ensure that user stories are independent, negotiable, valuable, estimable, small and testable.

Stories 8 and 11 were refined to make their expected behaviour clearer and easier to verify.
