## 2. Story Identified as Too Large

**Original Story 4:**

As a customer, I want to upload supporting documents so that I can provide evidence for my claim.

This story may involve different document types and upload scenarios, making it too large to estimate and complete easily.

## 3. Split into Vertical User Stories

| New Story | User Story |
|---|---|
| 4A | As a customer, I want to upload my primary claim document so that I can provide the essential evidence for my claim. |
| 4B | As a customer, I want to upload additional supporting documents so that I can provide further evidence for my claim. |

Each new story delivers a complete customer-facing document upload capability rather than splitting work into technical layers.

## 4. Conclusion

The original document upload story was identified as too large and divided into two smaller vertical user stories. The revised backlog contains 13 user stories that are easier to estimate, develop and test.
