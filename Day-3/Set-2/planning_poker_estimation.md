## 3. Planning Poker Discussion

Planning Poker is used to estimate the effort and complexity of each user story.

Each team member selects a Fibonacci value independently. The team discusses differences in estimates and agrees on a final story point value.

## 4. Outlier Discussion

**Story:** Submit Claim

- Initial estimates: 3, 5 and 8 points.
- Difference: Team members had different views about the validation and claim submission complexity.
- Discussion: The team discussed policy validation, incident details and claim reference generation.
- Final estimate: 5 story points.

## 5. Total Backlog Size
