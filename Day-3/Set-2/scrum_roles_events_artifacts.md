
# Scrum Roles, Events and Artifacts – Insurance Claims Portal

## 1. Scrum Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Product Owner | Defines the product vision, manages and prioritises the Product Backlog, and maximises product value. |
| Scrum Master | Ensures Scrum practices are followed, facilitates Scrum events and helps remove team obstacles. |
| Developers | Design, develop, test and deliver a usable product increment during each Sprint. |

## 2. Scrum Events

| Event | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Sprint | A fixed period during which the team works towards a Sprint Goal. |
| Sprint Planning | Defines the Sprint Goal and selects backlog items for the Sprint. |
| Daily Scrum | Helps Developers inspect progress towards the Sprint Goal and plan their work. |
| Sprint Review | Demonstrates the completed increment and gathers stakeholder feedback. |
| Sprint Retrospective | Identifies improvements for the next Sprint. |

## 3. Scrum Artifacts

| Artifact | Description |
|---|---|
| Product Backlog | Ordered list of features, improvements and requirements for the Insurance Claims Portal. |
| Sprint Backlog | Selected user stories and the plan to deliver them during a Sprint. |
| Increment | Completed and usable product functionality delivered at the end of a Sprint. |

## 4. Application to the Insurance Claims Portal

The Product Owner prioritises features such as Claim Registration, Document Upload, Claim Tracking and Settlement Notifications.

The Scrum Master facilitates Scrum events and helps resolve obstacles. Developers implement and test the selected user stories.

The team uses the Product Backlog to manage requirements, the Sprint Backlog to plan work and the Increment to deliver usable portal functionality.

## Conclusion

Scrum helps the Insurance Claims Portal team deliver features incrementally, collect regular feedback and continuously improve the product.
