
# Insurance Claims Portal

## 1. Product Vision

To develop a user-friendly self-service insurance claims portal that allows customers to register claims, upload supporting documents, track claim status and receive settlement notifications. The portal aims to improve transparency, reduce manual processing and provide faster claim services.

## 2. Waterfall vs Agile Comparison

| Waterfall | Agile |
|---|---|
| Follows a sequential development process. | Follows an iterative development process. |
| Requirements are defined at the beginning. | Requirements can change during development. |
| Testing is generally performed after development. | Testing is performed continuously. |
| Customer feedback usually comes at later stages. | Customer feedback is collected regularly. |
| Changes can be costly and difficult. | Changes can be accommodated more easily. |

## 3. Why Agile Suits This Programme

Agile is suitable for the Insurance Claims Portal because customer requirements and insurance processes may change. The team can develop features in small increments, collect feedback from customers and stakeholders, and improve the portal continuously. Features such as claim registration, document upload, claim tracking and settlement notifications can be developed and tested in separate sprints.
